Game Instructions
  • Controls: Click/touch and drag horizontally at the top, then release to drop the glowing ball.
  • Goal: Guide the ball into the glowing Orange Target Bucket to score +100 points.
  • Goal Persistence: The target bucket stays the same across rounds until you click "Reset Game" or manually change it.
  • Changing Target Position: Use the "Randomize Goal" button anytime in the top right to shift the orange target to a new location.
